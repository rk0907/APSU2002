About the Project

Building decent homes for the teachers who built us.

Why This Matters

Many of the teachers who shaped us at APSU still face challenges with accommodation. The Teachers’ Accommodation Project (Project BOLT) seeks to construct a modern 14-unit facility that will give educators safe, comfortable homes close to the school.

The Goal

Raise funds to complete a 14-unit teachers’ accommodation facility on campus.

Who We Are

We are the APSU 2002 Year Group — proud Old Boys and Girls who believe that supporting the teachers who once supported us is one of the most meaningful ways we can give back.

How You Can Help

Every contribution, no matter the size, moves us closer to the goal. Whether you are an APSUnian or a friend of the school, your support makes a real difference.
